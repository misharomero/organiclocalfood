About / Sobre Nosotros

Two Farms,
One Story

We are a family rooted in two places. In Tacoma, we grow vegetables, herbs, and fruit in the Pacific Northwest rain and sun. In El Salvador, we tend a farm that has fed our family for generations.

This site is where we share what we're learning — about soil, seasons, seeds, and the joy of growing your own food.

Somos una familia con raíces en dos lugares. Este sitio es donde compartimos lo que aprendemos — sobre la tierra, las estaciones, las semillas, y la alegría de cultivar tus propios alimentos.
