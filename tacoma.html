The Garden / El Jardín

Growing in the
Pacific Northwest

In Tacoma, Washington, we grow vegetables, herbs, and fruit through the Pacific Northwest seasons — long rainy winters, spectacular summers, and the particular joy of growing food in Zone 8b.

This spring we are focused on tomatoes — specialty varieties, started from seed indoors, now hardening off and finding their place in the garden.

En Tacoma, Washington, cultivamos verduras, hierbas y frutas a través de las estaciones del noroeste del Pacífico. Esta primavera nos enfocamos en los tomates — variedades especiales, iniciadas desde semilla adentro, ahora endureciéndose y encontrando su lugar en el jardín.
